FAQ

Do I need a reservation?
Reservations are not required, but they are recommended on Fridays and Saturdays. Call us at (613) 741-4657.
Do you deliver?
Take-out is available for pick-up only. Sorry, we do not deliver.
Are you licensed?
Yes, Pilos is fully licensed.
Do you have a kids menu?
Yes, for children under 12. Kids plates are served with green salad and french fries, and are not available for take-out.
Do you sell gift certificates?
Yes, gift certificates are available.
