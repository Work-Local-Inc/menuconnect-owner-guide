The work behind it

What work and thinking went into it?

We studied how the biggest ordering platforms handle checkout, speed, and order problems. We studied more than four million past orders to learn what customers add and where sales can be lost. Then we rebuilt the ordering path, screen by screen, and tested it on real devices and browsers.

The goal was simple: meet the standards customers expect, remove the parts that slow them down, and keep the restaurant in control of the order.
